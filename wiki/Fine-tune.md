Fine-tune is in [More Options](More-Options#advanced) → Advanced → Fine-tune.

The defaults work on most devices. Before reporting a problem, press **Reset to defaults** and check whether it still happens.
Tap a setting to see its default value.

## Support

| Setting | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swipes per Update | 10 | 0–35 | How often to scroll through the support list before refreshing. |
| Max Updates | 20 | 0–50 | How often to refresh the support list before using the [Fallback](Support-Selection#refreshing-and-fallback). |

## Similarity

How closely the screen must match an image. Only change these if something isn't detected, and only a little.

| Setting | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Min Similarity | 80% | 50–100% | Used for all image matching. |
| MLB Similarity | 70% | 50–100% | For the MLB star of support CEs. Lower it if MLB CEs aren't detected. |
| Stage Counter Similarity | 85% | 50–100% | For the wave counter. Change it if your commands are used in the wrong wave. |

## Clicks

| Setting | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wait after clicking | 300 ms | 50–2000 ms | Wait after each tap, for the game's animations, unless tapping repeatedly. |
| Click duration | 50 ms | 5–200 ms | How long each tap is held. |
| Multi-Click Interval | 10 ms | 5–50 ms | Time between taps when tapping repeatedly, like at the end of battles, in Friend Point Summon and Lottery. |

## Swipes

| Setting | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wait after swiping | 700 ms | 50–3000 ms | Wait after each swipe, for the game's animations. |
| Swipe duration | 300 ms | 50–1000 ms | How long a swipe takes. Faster swipes scroll further. |
| Swipe Multiplier | 100% | 50–200% | Scales the distance of each swipe. |

## Wait

| Setting | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skill Delay | 500 ms | 50–2000 ms | Wait between tapping a skill and tapping its target. |
| Wait before Turn | 500 ms | 50–2000 ms | Wait after the battle screen appears before using skills. |
| Wait before Cards | 2000 ms | 50–6000 ms | Wait after tapping Attack before picking cards and NPs. |
| Wait Multiplier | 100% | 50–200% | Scales every wait. Raise it to slow everything down on slow devices. |
