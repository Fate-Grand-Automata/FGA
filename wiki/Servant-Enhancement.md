Levels a Servant with embers until it reaches its level cap or you run out of embers or QP. It can also ascend the Servant and keep leveling.

## Starting

1. In FGO, go to Servant Enhancement and select the Servant to level.
2. Press PLAY.

You can also start from the Servant's Ascension screen.

FGA selects the embers with the game's **Auto Select** button, so it uses whatever embers the game picks.

## Options

- **Redirect to Ascension upon reaching the level cap:** at the level cap, open the Ascension screen.
- **Perform Ascension:** also ascend the Servant, then go back to leveling. Ascension with duplicate Servants isn't supported.
- **Redirect to Palingenesis upon reaching the level cap:** at the level cap, open the Palingenesis (Holy Grail) screen and stop there.

If both redirects are possible, Ascension wins.

## When it stops

- **Max Level Achieved:** the level cap is reached and no redirect applies.
- **Redirected to Ascension** or **Redirected to Palingenesis**
- **Insufficient Embers or QP**
- **Unable to perform Ascension**
- **No Servant Selected**
